OPTIONAL COST CONTROL

Ingredients & costs

Costing is optional. Products and orders work normally without ingredient prices or recipes. Update an input cost here whenever a supplier price changes; current product cost estimates recalculate automatically.

How costing works

  • Set each ingredient's price and price unit, for example R16.78 per kg or R4.00 per each.
  • Recipe quantities must use the same unit as the ingredient price unit.
  • Packaging cost is optional and is added per product.
  • Changing an ingredient price updates current product estimates. Saved orders retain their recorded selling price and order details.

Important things to know

  • Data is stored in this browser on this device, not in your GitHub account.
  • Private/incognito browsing is not suitable for keeping your bakery records.
  • Browser storage can be cleared. Export backups regularly and store them outside this device.
  • Importing a backup on another device copies the data there; it does not create live synchronisation.
  • WhatsApp message sending requires an internet connection and your WhatsApp account.
